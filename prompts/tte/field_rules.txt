TTE FIELD RULES

Compiled from 'KOS Guidelines for Name Construction in TTE', ver 5.1, 10 Sep 2026.
Field names are given exactly as they are stored in TTE, which differs from the
guidelines' own spelling in one place (Affiliations(groupName), no space).
Fields the pipeline cannot populate from a news article (URIs, images, sources,
coordinates, cataloguing-internal names) are omitted.

======================================================================
PERSON   (guidelines section 3.4, 'People')
======================================================================

Name   [mandatory field]
  - Create the preferred name based on the following criteria and in
    accordance with RDA standards (refer RDA section 9.0): a) Use the name
    by which the person is most commonly known in the local context, even
    if this differs from the LCNA established name. Exclude the birth and
    death date from the preferred name – these are to be placed under the
    attributes Birth Year and Death Year. b) The name is mentioned in at
    least two sources. c) An abbreviation or symbol is not to be
    established as a preferred name unless the name is commonly used.
  - Name with alias: If a person is more commonly known by an alias or
    nickname, establish this as the preferred name. If it cannot be
    determined if the alias is better known ( i.e., unable to find
    biographical information), then establish the birth name as the
    preferred name and the alias as a non-preferred name.
  - If a nickname forms part of the name and appears in brackets, include
    it in the preferred name. [This is most often found in NAS names of
    WWII POWs.]
  - For women, the married name and maiden name needs to be separated into
    PT and NPT. The more commonly used name shall be the PT. If it cannot
    be determined which is more commonly used, treat the maiden name as
    the PT .

Qualifier   [mandatory if applicable]
  - A qualifier must be added in the following conditions (Refer to RDA
    section 9.19.1.2 - 9.19.1.7 for details): a) The name does not convey
    the idea of a person. A qualifier is added to indicate the profession
    or occupation of the person. This also applies to a name that does not
    include a surname or consists only of initials. Examples: Name: Big
    Hand Qualifier: Musician Name: Soon Qualifier: Musician b) More than
    one person with the exact same name, with no Title to differentiate
    them. A qualifier must be added to distinguish one from the other. Use
    the following qualifiers in order of preference: i) fuller form of the
    name ii) occupation iii ) birth and/or death year iv) any other
    differentiator.

Title
  - Includes titles of royalty, nobility, religious rank, honour, or
    office . (Refer to RDA section 9.4 and 9.19.1.7 for details.)
  - Excludes: a) terms of address that simply indicate gender or marital
    status . The exception is when “Mrs” is used for a woman who is known
    only through her husband’s name. However, if a woman is using only her
    husband’s surname in place of her own, do not include “Mrs” as a title
    (see 3.4.1.4 above). Omit the fullstop in Mrs. b) titles for
    professions, military ranks and designations unless it is always used,
    or it is needed for disambiguation. When abbreviations for titles are
    used, omit the fullstop if both the first and last letter of the word
    are present, as in Dr or Lt, but include the fullstop otherwise, as in
    Prof.
  - Where a title is included, use the form given in Appendix 2 at the end
    of this file.

Description   [mandatory field]
  - The description for a person should be concise and limited to 3 to 5
    complete sentences. It should include the following information (in
    order of priority) wherever possible: a) Highlight the person's main
    occupation or what the person is most well known for. If a person is
    holding more than one position, highlight only the most significant
    and most recent one or two positions. Avoid merely listing
    designations like in a resume. b) Focus on the person being described.
    Do not dwell on his/her relatives and connections with other people.
  - Information in the description must be based on authoritative sources.
  - If the name of the person is derived from bibliographic records in
    GT.Net and information on the person is lacking, state the role of the
    person in relation to the bibliographic record, and the type of work,
    such as fiction, a collection of poetry, research paper or song. Add a
    note under the field “Note” in the following format - “Name derived
    from bib record UUID”, followed by the UUID of the bib record.
  - Description is not required for non -preferred names. Any description
    placed under non - preferred names should be transferred to the
    preferred names.
  - Refer to the style guide for descriptions in section 7.

Birth Year (yyyy)   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the year only (yyyy). The month and day go into their respective
    fields . Leave blank if the birth year is unavailable or in doubt.

Birth Month (mm)
  - Enter the month only (mm). The year and day go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the birth month is unavailable or in doubt.
    Enter the month only if the year has been filled in.

Birth Day (dd)
  - Enter the day only (dd). The year and month go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the birth day is unavailable or in doubt. Enter
    a day only if the year and month have been filled in.

Death Year (yyyy)   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the year only (yyyy). The month and day go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the death year is unavailable or in doubt.

Death Month (mm)
  - Enter the month only (mm). The year and day go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the death month is unavailable or in doubt.
    Enter the month only if the year has been filled in.

Death Day (dd)
  - Enter the day only (dd). The year and month go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the death day is unavailable or in doubt .
    Enter the day only if the year and month have been filled in.

Spouse
  - Refers to the person with a marital or civil union relationship to the
    person described. Enter the preferred name (PT) of the person, as
    found in TTE, LCNA or VIAF, in that order of preference. If the name
    is not found in any of these sources, enter it as you would in PT
    form.
  - If there is more than one spouse in different periods in time, include
    all names in chronological order, separated by a delimiter ( | ).
    Indicate the period of marriage in brackets, preceding the date with
    "m.": "Name (m. YYYY-YYYY)" once both the start and end are known,
    "Name (m. YYYY)" while the marriage is current. If the dates of an
    earlier marriage are unknown, write "(div.)" or "(deceased)" after that
    spouse's name instead of a date.
    Example 1: Spouse: Woon, Alice (m. 1942-1986) | Phua, Swee Liang (m.
    1991)
    Example 2: Spouse: Lam, May Yee (deceased) | Khoo, Shellen
  - If a name record has been created for the spouse, relate it using RT.

Child
  - Refers to the person who is a natural or legal offspring of the person
    described. Enter the preferred name (PT) of the person, as found in
    TTE, LCNA or VIAF, in that order of preference. If the name is not
    found in any of these sources, enter it as you would in PT form.
  - If there is more than one child, include all names and separate them
    with a delimiter ( | ).
  - If a name record has been created for the child, relate it using RT.

Parent
  - Refers to the person who is a natural or legal parent of the person
    described. Enter the preferred name (PT) of the person, as found in
    TTE, LCNA or VIAF, in that order of preference. If the name is not
    found in any of these sources, enter it as you would in PT form.
  - If more than one name is entered, separate the names with a delimiter
    ( | ).
  - If a name record has been created for the parent, relate it using RT.

Nationality
  - Refers to the nationality of the person described. Use the demonym
    instead of the country, i.e., use “Singaporean” and not “Singapore”.
  - Nationality may or may not be equivalent to birth place and death
    place.
  - Leave blank if in doubt.

Place of Birth   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter here the place of birth of the person, in the format: City or
    Town, Country
  - If the city or town is not known, then just the country will do.
  - Use Getty Thesaurus of Geographic Names (TGN) at
    http://www.getty.edu/research/tools/vocabularies/tgn/ for the place
    names. Choose the English preferred name (indicated with ‘English-P’)
    if the name is in more than one language.

Occupation   [mandatory if known]
  - This is the durable engagement the person described has in an activity
    or responsibility.
  - Use only the terms listed in the Occupations Thesaurus. If the term
    needed is not found, submit the term to the Chief Editor for
    consideration.
  - Exclude designations (e.g., Minister for Defence, CEO). These can go
    into the description if necessary.
  - If more than one occupation is entered, separate the occupations with
    a delimiter ( | ).

Active Years
  - The year range during which the person was active in his most
    prominent occupation.

Achievements
  - Refers to the person’s key achievements. List up to 3 most significant
    achievements associated with the person, described in 3 to 5 full
    sentences.
  - Achievements may include: a) Recognition as the first or top in
    Singapore or the world in certain areas. b) Places, organisations
    and/or awards named in honour of the person.
  - Achievements are NOT: a) A duplication of the description b) A list of
    the person’s designations or portfolio c) Awards the person has
    received, as these should go in the Awards field d) The person’s
    relationship with another well-known person e) Illegal acts committed
    by the person
  - Leave blank if the person has no significant achievements yet, or if
    not much information is known.

Education
  - Refers to the educational institution at which the person described
    received his/her education. Enter the preferred name (PT) of the
    educational institution, as found in TTE, LCNA or VIAF, in that order
    of preference. If the name is not found in any of these sources, enter
    it as you would in PT form.
  - If more than one institution is entered, separate the names with a
    delimiter ( | ).

Awards
  - Refers to the award(s) received by the person. Enter the name of the
    award, followed by the year awarded in brackets, if available.
  - If more than one award is entered, separate the awards with a
    delimiter ( | ).

Affiliations(groupName)   [mandatory if available]
  - Refers to the key organisation(s) with which the person described is
    affiliated or is most often associated with. Enter the preferred name
    (PT) of the organisation, as found in TTE, LCNA or VIAF, in that order
    of preference. If the name is not found in any of these sources, enter
    it as you would in PT form.
  - Exclude the educational institutions which the person attended, as
    these would be placed in the Education field.
  - If more than one organisation is entered, separate the names with a
    delimiter ( | ).

ORGANISATION   (guidelines section 3.3, 'Organisations')
======================================================================

Name   [mandatory field]
  - Create the preferred name based on the following criteria and in
    accordance with RDA standards (refer RDA section 11.0): a) Use the
    officially established name. This means the official name that the
    organisation wishes to be known as, as stated in the official website
    and documents.
  - If the officially established name contradicts with names derived from
    alternate sources, verify whether it is a typo error on the part of
    the creating agency. Alert and clarify with the creating agency if
    necessary.
  - When an organisation is renamed, create a new entry for the new name
    and link them with a predecessor-successor relationship.
  - When organisations are merged or split, use RT. The merging
    organisations should have a RT link with the merged organisation, but
    not with each other. Similarly, when an organisation is split, it
    should have RT links to the split organisations, but the spl it
    organisations should not be related to each other.

Qualifier   [mandatory if applicable]
  - A qualifier must be added in the following conditions (Refer to RDA
    section 11.13.1.2 - 11.13.1.8 for details): a) When the name does not
    convey the idea of an organisation , or is a transliteration, a
    qualifier is added to indicate the nature of the organisation.
  - However, note the following: a) Administrative information such as
    "expunged" and "defunct" is not considered to be a qualifier.

Description   [mandatory field]
  - The description for organisation names should be concise and limited
    to 3 to 5 complete sentences. It should include the following
    information (in order of priority) wherever possible: a) The nature of
    the organisation (commercial company, government agency, etc.). b) The
    main roles and functions of the organisation. c) Specify any changes
    in role and function, changes in name and the year the changes took
    place.
  - Information in the description should be based on authoritative
    sources.
  - If the name of the entity is derived from bibliographic records in
    GT.Net and information on the entity is lacking, state the role of the
    entity in relation to the bibliographic record. Add a note under the
    field “Note” in the following format - “Name derived from bib record
    UUID”, followed by the UUID of the bib record.
  - Descriptions are not required for non -preferred names. Any
    description placed under non - preferred names should be transferred
    to the preferred name.
  - Refer to the style guide for descriptions in section 7.

Street Address   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the street address of the organisation. The format of the
    address should be: <street number> <street name>, <#unit-number>
    <building name>. Spell the street name in full and do not use
    abbreviations such as Rd, St, Dr. A former address may be placed under
    description if it is important.
  - Leave blank if the address is unknown or the organisation no longer
    exists.

Country   [mandatory field]
  - Enter Singapore, even if the organisation no longer exists. Only names
    of Singapore organisations should be created in TTE.

Postal Code   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the postal code of the organisation.
  - Leave blank if the organisation no longer exists.

Founder   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the name(s) of the founder of the organisation. Enter the
    preferred name (PT) of the person, as found in TTE, LCNA or VIAF, in
    that order of preference. If the name is not found in any of these
    sources, enter it as you would in PT form. Use a delimiter ( | ) to
    separate names if there is more than one name.

Year Started (yyyy)   [mandatory if available]
  - Refers to any of the following: a) Year of establishment of the
    organisation.
  - Enter the year (yyyy) only. The month and day go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the precise year started is not available.

Month Started (mm)
  - Refers to any of the following: a) Month of establishment of the
    organisation. b) Month of name change. This should be highlighted in
    the description. c) Month of merger between two or more organisations.
    This should be highlighted in the description.
  - Enter the month (mm) only. The year and day go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the precise month started is not available. The
    month should be entered only if the year has been filled in.

Day Started (dd)
  - Refers to any of the following: a) Day of establishment of the
    organisation. b) Day of name change. This should be highlighted in the
    description. c) Day of merger between two or more organisations. This
    should be highlighted in the description.
  - Enter the day (dd) only. The month and year go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the precise day started is not available. The
    day should be entered only if the year and month have been filled in.

Year Ended (yyyy)   [mandatory if available]
  - Refers to any of the following: a) The year in which the organisation
    ended. See example at 3.3.11.1 a. b) Year of name change of the
    organisation. This should be highlighted in the description and a
    Predecessor-Successor relationship added between the preceding and
    succeeding names. See example at 3.3.11.1 b. c) Year of merger or
    split between organisations. This should be highlighted in the
    description and Related Terms (RT) added between the organisations.
  - Enter the year (yyyy) only. The month and day go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the precise year ended is not available.

Month Ended (mm)
  - Refers to any of the following: a) The month in which the organisation
    ended. b) Month of name change. This should be highlighted in the
    description. c) Month of merger between two or more organisations.
    This should be highlighted in the description.
  - Enter the month (mm) only. The year and day go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the precise month ended is not available. The
    month should be entered only if year has been filled in.

Day Ended (dd)
  - Refers to any of the following: a) The day on which the organisation
    ended. b) Day of name change. This should be highlighted in the
    description. c) Day of merger or split between organisations. This
    should be highlighted in the description.
  - Enter the day (dd) only. The month and year go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the precise day ended is not available. The day
    should be entered only if the year and month have been filled in.

Parent Organisation   [mandatory if available]
  - The parent organisation is the organisation that controls the
    described entity. Enter the preferred name of the parent organisation.
    Information on past parent organisation(s) may be included in the
    description, if important.
  - Add a Broader Term (BT) relationship to the parent organisation for
    current entities, but not if the described entity no longer exists .
    If the described entity has been succeeded by another, remove the BT
    relationship such that the parent organisation only has its current
    NTs.

Group Members
  - This is mainly applicable for members of small organisations such as
    musical groups and art collectives. Enter the preferred name (PT) of
    the person, as found in TTE, LCNA or VIAF, in that order of
    preference. If the name is not found in any of these sources, enter it
    as you would in PT form. Use a delimiter ( | ) to separate the names.

Awards
  - Refers to the awards received by the organisation.
  - Enter name of the award, followed by the year awarded in brackets if
    available. If more than one award is entered, separate the awards with
    a delimiter ( | ).

FACILITY   (guidelines section 3.1, 'GeoBuildings')
======================================================================

Name   [mandatory field]
  - Create the preferred name based on the following criteria and in
    accordance with RDA (refer to RDA section 16.2): a) Use the officially
    established name of the building. A search for the address in OneMap
    should yield the building name. Take care not to accord an
    organisation’s name to a building just because the organisation
    occupies the building. Building names in Singapore are approved by the
    Street and Building Names Board (SBNB). The SBNB provides a search
    service for approved street and building names at Search for Approved
    Names and Addresses - Property Name and Address e-Service
    (propertynaa.gov.sg) which also provides the Chinese translation of
    the name. Note however, that it does not include buildings that no
    longer exist. b) The building name should be mentioned in at least two
    sources. OneMap is a reliable source. c) Avoid mere transliteration or
    Romanisation of a non -English name ( such as the use of hanyu
    pinyin), unless that form of name has been officially recognised, or
    where an official translation is absent.
  - If the officially established name contradicts with names derived from
    alternate sources, verify if it is a typo error on the part of the
    creating agency. Alert and clarify with the creating agency if
    necessary.
  - When a building is renamed, change the preferred name to the new name,
    and establish the old name as a non-preferred name.

Qualifier
  - If there are buildings sharing the same name in different parts of
    Singapore, a qualifier specifying the location is required to
    disambiguate between the entities.
  - If the building name is generic or common, and is likely to exist in
    other countries, qualify it with “Singapore”.
  - If the building name does not convey the idea of a building, include a
    qualifier such as “Mall”. For Chinese temples, if the name is in
    Chinese and does not include the word “Temple”, insert “Temple” as a
    qualifier.
  - If the building name is also the name of an organisation, qualify the
    building name with “Place”. Also note para 2.2 to avoid creating a
    building name that is the same as an organisation name unless the
    building has historical significance. It is unnecessary to use the
    “Place” qualifier if there is no organisation of the same name.
  - However, do note the following: a) Administrative information such as
    "expunged" and "defunct" should not be used as a qualifier.

Description   [mandatory field]
  - The description for building names should be concise and limited to 3
    to 5 complete sentences. It should focus on the building and not the
    occupants . It should include the following information (in order of
    priority) wherever possible: a) Functions of the building past and
    present. Specify that it is a n office building, shopping mall, etc.
    b) The year it was revamped, repurposed or demolished, or the year the
    building name was changed (if applicable). c) Former and current
    tenants and/or owners of the building, if significant. d) If
    significant and pertinent, the name of the builder or designer of the
    building, and details on the architecture style, design, etc. of the
    building may be included.
  - Information in the description must be based on authoritative sources.
  - If the name of the entity is derived from bibliographic records in
    GT.Net and information on the entity is lacking, state the role of the
    entity in relation to the bibliographic record. Add a note under the
    field “Note” in the following format - “Name derived from bib record
    UUID”, followed by the UUID of the bib record.
  - Descriptions are not required for non -preferred names. Any
    description placed under non - preferred names should be transferred
    to the preferred name.
  - Refer to the style guide for descriptions in section 7.

Street Address   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the street address of the building. The format should be street
    number followed by street name. Spell the street name in full and do
    not use abbreviations such as Rd, St, Dr. There should not be any unit
    number as the entity should not be a subset of the whole building. The
    exception is for libraries, museums and mosques, as some are housed
    within buildings.
  - Leave blank if the building no longer exists and the address is
    unknown.

Country   [mandatory field]
  - Enter Singapore. Only buildings in Singapore should be created in TTE.
    Do not leave blank.

Postal Code   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the postal code of the building. The postal code can be found on
    OneMap using the building name or street address.
  - Leave blank if the building no longer exists.

Feature Type   [mandatory if applicable]
  - This is included for readability of the feature code. It refers to the
    geographical feature listed in GeoNames
    (http://www.geonames.org/export/codes.html).
  - Input the feature type exactly as it appears in GeoNames, in lower
    case and using the American spelling. However, always adopt the
    singular form when GeoNames includes the plural form in brackets as an
    option. For example: for feature code BLDG, the feature type is given
    as “building(s)”; always input “building”. The exception is when the
    plural form functions as the singular, as in barracks and waterworks.

Year Completed (yyyy)   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the year only (yyyy). The month and day go into their respective
    fields. Leave blank if the year of completion of the building is
    unavailable or in doubt.

Month Completed (mm)   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the month only (mm). Leave blank if the month of completion of
    the building is unavailable or in doubt. Valid only if year has been
    entered.

Day Completed (dd)   [mandatory if available]
  - Enter the day only (dd). Leave blank if the day of completion of the
    building is unavailable or in doubt. Valid only if year and month have
    been filled in.

LOCATION   (guidelines section 3.2, 'Geographics')
======================================================================

Name   [mandatory field]
  - Create the preferred name based on the following criteria and in
    accordance with RDA (refer to RDA section 16.2): a) Use the officially
    established name. Street names in Singapore are approved by the Street
    and Building Names Board (SBNB). The SBNB provides a search service
    for approved street and building names at Search for Approved Names
    and Addresses - Property Name and Address e-Service
    (propertynaa.gov.sg) which also provides the Chinese translation of
    the name. It does not cover streets and estates that no longer exist.
    Spell the street name in full and do not use abbreviations such as Rd,
    St, Dr. b) The name should be mentioned in at least two sources.
    OneMap is a reliable source. c) Avoid mere transliteration or
    Romanisation of a non -English name ( such as the use of hanyu
    pinyin), unless that form of name has been officially recognised, or
    where an official translation is absent.
  - If the officially established name contradicts with names derived from
    alternate sources, verify if it is a typo error on the part of the
    creating agency. Alert and clarify with the creating agency if
    necessary.
  - When a place is renamed, change the preferred name to the new name and
    establish the old name as a non-preferred name.

Qualifier
  - If there are places sharing the same name in different parts of
    Singapore, a qualifier specifying the location is required to
    disambiguate between the entities.
  - However, do note the following: a) Administrative information such as
    "expunged" and "defunct" is not considered to be a qualifier.
  - c. d) A name which includes hierarchical relationship and non
    -preferred names should not be established as a qualifier. They should
    be established as separate records. See example at

Description   [mandatory field]
  - The description for place names should be concise and limited to 3 to
    5 complete sentences. It should include the following information (in
    order of priority) wherever possible: a) Specify the nature of the
    geographical entity, such as a historical site, a hill or a park. b)
    The location of the entity. c) The year in which the place or road
    name first appeared or was named , and the year it was demolished or
    renamed (if applicable). d) Avoid long explanatory notes on the
    origins of the place names. e) If historically significant, the name
    of the builder or designer of bridges or roads , details on the
    architecture style, design, etc. of the bridges or roads may be
    included.
  - Information in the description must be based on authoritative sources.
  - If the name of the entity is derived from bibliographic records in
    GT.Net and information on the entity is lacking, state the role of the
    entity in relation to the bibliographic record. Add a note under the
    field “Note” in the following format - “Name derived from bib record
    UUID”, followed by the UUID of the bib record.
  - Scope notes are not required for non -preferred names. Any
    descriptions placed under non - preferred names should be transferred
    to the description field of the preferred name.
  - Refer to the style guide for descriptions in section 7.

Country   [mandatory field]
  - Enter “Singapore”. Only Singapore place names are be created in TTE.

Feature Type   [mandatory if applicable]
  - This is included for readability of the feature code. It refers to the
    geographical feature listed in GeoNames
    (http://www.geonames.org/export/codes.html).
  - Input the feature type exactly as it appears in GeoNames, in lower
    case and using the American spelling. However, always adopt the
    singular form when GeoNames includes the plural form in brackets as an
    option. For example: for feature code RSV, the feature type is given
    as “reservoir(s)”; always input “reservoir”.

EVENT   (guidelines section 3.5, 'Historical Events')
======================================================================

Name   [mandatory field]
  - As the rules for identifying and naming of events in RDA are not yet
    available, the name has been created according to the Library of
    Congress Subject Headings Manual format of “[name of event], [name of
    jurisdiction or locality], [date or date span]”, where date is the
    year. The name of jurisdiction may be omitted if it is already in the
    name of the event. (Refer to Library of Congress Subject Headings
    Manual, H1596, p 3-4.)
  - Establish any other names the event is known by as non-preferred
    terms.

Category   [mandatory field]
  - Enter a subject from SingHeritage Events.
  - If there is more than one subject, separate them with a delimiter ( |
    ).

Topic   [mandatory field]
  - Enter a Library of Congress Subject Heading, including the appropriate
    subdivisions.
  - If there is more than one subject, separate them with a delimiter ( |
    ).

Description   [mandatory field]
  - Describe the event concisely in 3 to 5 complete sentences. Begin with
    the Category from 3.5.2. above. Include the following: a) The nature
    of the event, what caused it and its consequences b) The exact dates
    when the event occurred , if available, especially if it took place
    within a year.

Locality
  - Enter the exact place, area or district where the event took place.
    Enter the preferred name (PT) of the place, as found in TTE. If the
    name is not found, enter it as you would in PT form.
  - Enter the year only (yyyy).

Year Ended   [mandatory field]

======================================================================
AWARD, PROGRAMME, LEGAL_ACT   (guidelines section 3.6, thesaurus terms)
======================================================================

These three are thesaurus terms rather than full vocabulary records, so the
guidelines define only Term and SN for them. The fields below are the ones
these records actually carry in TTE.

AWARD
  - SN   the scope note: what the award is, who confers it, what it is for.
  - Awarding Entity   the organisation that confers it.
  - Year First Awarded   the year only (yyyy).

PROGRAMME
  - Description   what the programme is and who runs it.

LEGAL_ACT
  - SN   the scope note: what the act covers.

EVENT
  - SN   the scope note describing the event.
  - Organiser   the organisation running it.
  - Year, Month, Day   the date it occurred, as separate numeric fields.
  - Year Started, Year End   for events spanning more than one year.
  - Category, Topic, Locality, Description   as for Historical Events above.


======================================================================
STYLE GUIDE FOR DESCRIPTIONS   (guidelines section 7)
======================================================================

Every Description field above points here.

  - Paraphrase; never copy wording from the source.
  - Pay attention to grammar and spelling. Use British spelling.
  - Spell out single-digit numbers (one, two, three); use numerals from 10
    (10, 11, 12).
  - Avoid unspecific words that date the text: "current", "former", "then",
    "now".
  - Stay objective. No subjective phrasing ("attractive enclave",
    "disgusting place") and no ranking claims ("best in Asia", "largest in
    the world").
  - Exclude quantitative information that may change over time (e.g. "the
    company has 100 employees").
  - Avoid listing the positions a person has held, like a resume.
  - Focus on the entity being described.


======================================================================
APPENDIX 2: ABBREVIATIONS USED IN TTE
======================================================================

Use these forms for consistency. By convention (British English usage), an
abbreviation omits the full stop when the first and last letter of the word
are both present.

  Associate Professor -> Assoc. Prof.
  Doctor               -> Dr
  Father                -> Fr
  Hajah                 -> Hjh
  Haji                  -> Hj.
  Junior                -> Jr
  Lieutenant            -> Lt
  Professor             -> Prof.
  Reverend              -> Rev.
  Reverend Father       -> Rev. Fr
  Sister                -> Sr
  Venerable             -> Ven.
  Bin                   -> bin (lower case)
  Binte                 -> bte (lower case)
  Saint                 -> St (never for "street" -- spell street names out)
  Company               -> Co.
  Corporation           -> Corp.
  Limited               -> Ltd
  Private Limited       -> Pte Ltd
